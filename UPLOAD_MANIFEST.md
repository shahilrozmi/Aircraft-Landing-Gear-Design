## Retained supporting analyses

The repository intentionally keeps unique trade, audit, sizing, FEA-handoff, convergence, and CAD-variant records when they provide engineering traceability, even when they are not the final/current design.

This includes:

- `phase1_model_development/` as a clearly labeled historical Phase 1 archive
- B1–B12 design trade history
- B13/B13F geometry-development variants and reports
- B14 brace, horn/clevis, packaging, and CAD handoff records
- B14B-10 contact/setup diagnostics
- B14B-11 R4/R6/R8 radius-trade and convergence diagnostics
- R8 local-submodel construction and validation records
- F2/F3 redesign-candidate CAD and screening records
- documentation backfill / traceability audit outputs that improve the engineering record
