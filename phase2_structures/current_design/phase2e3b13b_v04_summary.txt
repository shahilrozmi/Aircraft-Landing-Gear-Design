SELECTION REASON
- Ø50 through-bore matches the bronze sleeve OD and eliminates exposed Ø38 aluminum.
- It avoids an internal bore shoulder at the bushing ends.
- It gives 6 mm radial clearance between the Ø38 shaft and 7075 center passage.
- Relative to the old Ø38 bore, the centered-section area falls about 11.6%,
  Iy about 5.0%, and Iz about 2.7%; these are acceptable to TAKE TO FEA, not final approval.
- The minimum vertical ligament becomes 14 mm, so B13C local head/contact FEA is mandatory.

B13C CAD INTENT
- Machine/model one Ø50 passage through the head.
- Install one 30 mm-long flanged bronze bushing from each side.
- Leave the center 46 mm of the Ø50 passage unoccupied.
- Shaft is Ø38 nominal; it contacts bronze ID surfaces only, never 7075.
- Keep the Ø60 x 3 mm flange working geometry from B13B V0.3.
- Do not add a central keeper hole/keyway through the Ø38 shaft.
- Mx remains on the independent brace path.
