SELECTION REASON
- Static axial thrust pressure is extremely low relative to the preliminary bronze screen.
- Ø60 gives a 5 mm radial positive seat outside the Ø50 sleeve bore.
- 3 mm flange thickness leaves 6.5 mm of the current 9.5 mm external gap.
- Thickness is a PROVISIONAL packaging/FEA starting value; static face pressure does not size it.
- Flange-root bending, local 7075 face stress, washer detail, wear and fatigue remain for FEA/detail design.

--- RESULTING B13C GEOMETRY INTENT ---
Per side bronze bushing: sleeve Ø50 OD x Ø38 nominal ID x 30 mm long
                         integral flange Ø60 OD x 3 mm thick
Two bushings are inserted from opposite sides of the head.
The flange is the positive axial seat; the shaft shoulder/thrust washer acts on the bronze flange.
The center of the head must NOT retain an exposed Ø38 aluminum land that can touch the shaft.

--- IMPORTANT OPEN ITEM FOR CAD ---
Central shaft clearance/relief diameter is NOT frozen in V0.3.
For B13C geometry, either keep the full head passage relieved to the Ø50 bushing OD,
or deliberately size a smaller central relief bore > Ø38 after a clearance/tolerance study.
Do NOT leave the old Ø38 7075 bore exposed between the two bushings.
