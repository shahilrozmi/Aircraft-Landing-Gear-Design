CHECK THE PRESSURE DIRECTION:
    Under internal pressure the bore should tend to expand radially.
    If it contracts, the pressure sign/orientation is wrong.

DO NOT:
    add pressure thrust if you later explicitly model a pressurized end cap.
    That would double-count the closed-end pressure load.

MESH — FIRST PASS
-----------------
Use quadratic solid elements.

Suggested starting mesh:
    general body size: ~6 mm
    transition refinement: ~2 mm

Then refine transition region to ~1 mm.

Near target A ~ 375 mm:
    wall thickness is about 5.889 mm.

Aim for multiple quadratic element layers through the wall.
Do not accept a peak located at the fixed face or remote coupling edge
as a transition stress result.
